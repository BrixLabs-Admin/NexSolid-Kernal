# Code of Conduct

NexSolid welcomes people of different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique technical ideas and code, not people.
- Help newcomers reproduce problems and learn the development workflow.
- Respect contributors' time, intellectual property, and attribution.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publishing private information without consent, or persistent disruptive conduct are not welcome.

## Reporting and moderation
For now, report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using a private GitHub contact channel where available. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. The owner will review reports, may remove inappropriate content, and may restrict participation. A dedicated private reporting address should be established before broader community recruitment.

This is a project conduct policy, not a substitute for GitHub's platform rules.
